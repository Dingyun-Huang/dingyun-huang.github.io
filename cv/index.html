  • 2024.01.17
    A database of thermally activated delayed fluorescent molecules auto-generated from scientific literature with ChemDataExtractor
    Springer Nature
    A database of thermally activated delayed fluorescent (TADF) molecules was automatically generated from the scientific literature. It consists of 25,482 data records with an overall precision of 82%. Among these, 5,349 records have chemical names in the form of SMILES strings which are represented with 91% accuracy; these are grouped in a subsidiary database. The databases have been publicly released for experimental and computational applications in the TADF research field.
  • 2022.08.26
    Evolution of one-dimensional electronic dynamics in Nb2⁢𝑛+1⁢Si𝑛⁢Te4⁢𝑛+2
    American Physical Society
    The electronic dynamics in Nb2⁢𝑛+1⁢Si𝑛⁢Te4⁢𝑛+2 with varying component 𝑛 are systematically investigated by low-temperature scanning tunneling microscopy/spectroscopy (STM/STS). Our quasiparticle interferences reveal a nondecay standing wave at the Fermi level, proving the one-dimensional (1D) nature of the charge carriers (holes) in the materials. The energy and component dependence of phase relaxation length 𝑙𝜙 and lifetime 𝜏 of 1D holes are unveiled and found to be consistent with the Tomonaga-Luttinger liquid theory. Our work demonstrates Nb2⁢𝑛+1⁢Si𝑛⁢Te4⁢𝑛+2 as a platform to manipulate 1D physics and offers an alternative perspective for studying electronic dynamics of non-Fermi liquids through STM experiments.
